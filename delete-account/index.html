Delete your Serenity Rising account

Serenity Rising: Idle RPG · by Shobel LLC

You can delete your Serenity Rising account and its data at any time, in the game or by email.

In the game

  1. Open the game and tap your profile picture.
  2. Open Settings, then under Account tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm twice. Your account is deleted right away and the game starts over.

During the story opening at the start of the game, the same Delete Account button is in the gear menu at the top of the screen.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Email shobelllc@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and your Player ID (shown in the game under Profile → Back Up). So that nobody can delete someone else's account, we may ask you to confirm it is yours, for example with your recovery code. We delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and Player ID
  • Your game progress and cloud saves
  • Your chat messages, guild membership and Arena team
  • Your Sign in with Apple link, if you used it (we also revoke the game's access with Apple)
  • Gameplay analytics and crash reports linked to your account

What is kept

Records of purchases (what was bought, when, and its price) are kept with no link to you or your account, as needed for refunds, tax and accounting. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund purchases; refunds go through Google Play or the App Store.
